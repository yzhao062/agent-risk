## 4. Preconditions: How the Offline Numbers Were Computed

All scripts and outputs are archived in `docs/agent-risk-modeling/agent-io-archive-2026-10-04/step2-offline/`. Each imports the frozen harness files read only; their hashes at run time were `analyze_replication.py` 9136bd6b... and `analyze_pilot.py` 0861f257..., the registered values.

**P1, B1 certification rows** (`compute_p1_p2.py`, output `p1_p2_results.json`). The script builds B1's rows exactly as `analyze_replication.main()` does for `--b1` (`outcomes(..., "replay")` on `logs/arms-cd/b1/episodes.jsonl`) and applies the section (v) code that D58 applied to B2: the worst-case compatible harm count, then the exact one-sided 95% Clopper-Pearson bound. As a check, the same function on B2 reproduces D58's (v) exactly (39, 0.0506; 94, 0.1106).

**Finding that the coordinator must settle.** Under the D58 worst-case rule, B1's full-record bound is 12/300 = 0.064, not the 11/300 = 0.060 that rule B1.3 reported (ARMS-CD; register D58 Section 0.4). The difference is one episode, `b1-0006`, whose environment-state checks failed (`check_errors` 4): its harm is unknown under the D58 rule and is counted as harmed, while rule B1.3 took its recorded harm (0) as known. Under the B1.3 convention the rows are 11 (0.0600) and 36 (0.1554) (`b13_convention.py`, `b13_convention_output.json`). The manuscript uses the D58 rule throughout (0.064 and 0.159), because P1 names that rule, the three rows must share one rule, and the conservative count is the one a certification should use; Appendix H states the 0.060 and 0.155 values and the reason. If the coordinator prefers rule B1.3, four places change: abstract, contributions, 4.2, Tables 2 and 3, and the H paragraph.

**P2, the exact (A3) coefficient on B2** (same script). Of 1,000 B2 episodes, 380 are stopped at θ = 0.94 and 372 of these have no harm before the stop, with no unknown outcome. Harm is binary and a stopped episode with earlier harm is already harmed, so replayed P(harm) under a post-stop harm probability p is exactly 0.039 + 0.372p. On B1 the coefficient is 137/300 = 0.457 (138 if the unknown episode counts); on B0 it is 106/300 = 0.353, which reproduces the register's D36 line.

**Appendix H values recomputed post hoc** (`compute_appendix_h.py`, output `appendix_h_values.json`): the B0 (A3) coefficients and kill-switch values (0.133, 0.123, 0.110 at θ = 0.99, 0.94, 0.19, matching the plan's figures), the kill switch on B2 (113/1000) and B1 (41/300), and the ablation that discards terminal contributions on B0 (kept 209, 189, 151; P(failure) 0.818, 0.799, 0.748; mean tokens 70,081, 67,791, 60,845), all matching PLAN-EXPERIMENTS D1 "Measured", now under checker v1.1.
