## Headline

The announced live abort rate is 181/400 = 0.4525 and the unannounced live abort rate is 153/400 = 0.3825, on the same 400 tasks and episode seeds. The paired difference is +0.070, Newcombe paired 95% [0.019, 0.120]. The interval excludes 0. Under the registered outcome map, this is evidence that the exact announcement-prompt intervention changes the marginal live abort probability, conditional on the execution-stability assumptions. It rejects the abort-rate implication of (A2) for this intervention; it does not identify semantic anticipation separately from prompt-length or other prompt perturbation effects. Reported independently, the equivalence test fails: the 90% interval [0.027, 0.112] is not inside (−0.03, 0.03). P(harm) is 10/400 = 0.025 in all three conditions.
