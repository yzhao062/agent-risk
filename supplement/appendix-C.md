\paragraph{Off-policy evaluation and dynamic regimes.} Off-policy evaluation and dynamic treatment regimes hold the closest precursors of Proposition~\ref{prop:replay}. Replay keeps logged events that match the target policy under randomized logging \citep{li2011unbiased}. Dynamic-regime comparisons censor at deviation \citep{cain2010start}. In optimal stopping, full trajectories evaluate any stopping rule with a known reward \citep{goel2017sample}. In our setting the logged gate is deterministic and never stops an episode, so no logged stop exists to reweight. A stop needs no overlap, as the agent never acts after it. Our stop term therefore rests on a declared abort valuation that keeps the loss accrued before the stop, (A3), and display~\eqref{eq:split} adds the continuing term.

\paragraph{Bounds without overlap.} Without overlap, partial identification gives sets \citep{manski2003partial}. \citet{khan2024offpolicy} bound one decision's unidentified term under smoothness. Sharp bounds for dynamic regimes leave unsupported cells free \citep{alvarez2026sharp}, and polynomial programs give sharp bounds in discrete causal models \citep{duarte2023automated}. The unsupported part of Proposition~\ref{prop:supported} specializes such bounds to replacement kernels with declared values, and gives an outer set when one law is shared across steps. \citet{zhang2020designing} bound dynamic regimes under unobserved confounding given a causal diagram, whose role our declared kernel inputs play. We bound missing support at unvisited inputs, with a declared terminal valuation. With deficient support in off-policy bandits, \citet{sachdeva2020offpolicy} restrict the action space, extrapolate rewards, or restrict the policy class; our unsupported term keeps such inputs free and bounds them. \citet{namkoong2020offpolicy} bound sequential policy values when unobserved confounding enters one decision; our bounds concern missing support with recorded decision inputs.

\paragraph{Transport and evidence collection.} \citet{bareinboim2016causal} give graphical conditions for transporting findings across domains; (A1), (A2), and (T) play that role between logs, restored runs, and the changed system. \citet{hanna2017dataefficient} design a behavior policy that lowers the error of evaluating a target policy. Proposition~\ref{prop:budget} instead decides how many unresolved continuations to execute, by uniform sampling with a finite-sample bound. Restoring logged states and running only the open continuations is a case of planning with reset access to visited states \citep{yin2022efficient} and of combining offline data with online interaction \citep{song2023hybrid}. A learned world model can instead simulate an LLM agent's environment for off-policy evaluation \citep{liu2026autoregressive}; its validity then rests on the model, where targeted execution rests on (T).

\paragraph{What logs must keep.} Agent logs \citep{chan2024visibility} and reasoning from a deployed system's logs \citep{bottou2013counterfactual} motivate Proposition~\ref{thm:logging}. It instantiates the elementary fact that a log determines an expected contribution for every path law exactly when indistinguishable paths have equal contributions. State-abstraction irrelevance gives sufficient conditions for evaluation on compressed states \citep{hao2024deeply}. Information states preserve values through reward and transition sufficiency \citep{subramanian2020ais}. Proposition~\ref{thm:logging} asks for a condition over the class of all baseline laws. Over that class, a log must preserve each episode's replay contribution, so the logging design decides which changes a deployer can later evaluate without running them.

\paragraph{Shared randomness and model comparison.} Shared-seed replay is a common-random-numbers coupling of the two policies. \citet{oberst2019counterfactual} infer Gumbel-max noise under a structural causal model to evaluate a new policy on logged episodes, and Gumbel-max coupling of language models generates counterfactual text and compares models \citep{chatzi2025counterfactual,ravfogel2025gumbel,corvelobenz2025coupled}. Seed-addressable replay reads recorded seeds instead, and Corollary~\ref{cor:model-update} needs only deterministic evaluation at logged inputs and seeds.

\paragraph{Agent evaluation and control.} \citet{ray2026enforced} bounds a multi-judge gate's errors by Fr\'echet--Hoeffding. Our range conditions on the state that composed agents share and transfers standalone evidence only on covered states (Proposition~\ref{prop:component-joint}). \citet{bhardwaj2026agent} certify the reliability of composed agents without assuming independence, from measured joint executions; our range needs only component logs and the shared state. \citet[Proposition~2 and Corollary~2]{ray2026enforced} also shows that ungated trajectories and static judge ROC need not identify a blocking regime's safety frontier. We separate stopping replay from continuing changes and state the evidence each requires. Agent-risk taxonomies and governance frameworks list the characteristics of Section~\ref{sec:characteristics} \citep[e.g.,][]{chan2023harms,slattery2024ai}. We add what each means for evaluation from logs.
